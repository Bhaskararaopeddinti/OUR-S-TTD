📋 Medical Guidelines

  • Emergency care is free for all pilgrims
  • Carry your ID and darshan token
  • For serious conditions, go directly to Aswini Hospital
  • First aid available at VQC and PAC complexes
  • Ambulance service available 24/7
  • Pharmacy available near temple area
  • Keep emergency numbers handy

💡 Health Tips for Pilgrims

💧 Stay hydrated - drink plenty of water
🧴 Use sunscreen and wear a hat
👟 Wear comfortable walking shoes
🍎 Eat light meals at Annaprasadam
😴 Take rest breaks during long queues
