📋 Accommodation Guidelines

  • PAC accommodation is free for all pilgrims
  • Valid darshan token required for PAC
  • Check-in time: 24/7 (subject to availability)
  • Maximum stay: 24 hours (PAC)
  • Paid guest houses require advance booking
  • Carry ID proof at all times
  • Maintain cleanliness and discipline
  • Report any issues to TTD staff

📋 Booking Information

PAC Booking: On-site (first-come, first-served)
Guest House Booking: Online at ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in
Booking Helpline: 1800-425-4141
Documents Required: ID proof, Darshan token
